chapter two · a letter from Minhal

To a beautiful girl Romi.

Some feelings deserve their own little envelope.

My dearest girl,

Happy 20th birthday to someone who means so much more to me than the word cousin could ever explain. You are family, yes — but you are also a sister my heart got to choose.

If I could wish one thing for you, it would be that life is gentle with your heart. I hope you find reasons to smile on ordinary days, people who love you in ways that feel safe, dreams that make you excited to wake up, and the courage to walk away from anything that makes you forget your worth.

I hope twenty brings you beautiful beginnings, unexpected happiness, peaceful nights, loud laughter, and moments that make you think, “I am so lucky to be here.” And on the days when life feels heavy, I hope you remember that you never have to be okay all by yourself. You have me — for the happy stories, the messy ones, the random talks, and all the little things in between.

I can't promise that every day will be perfect, but I will always wish for your happiness with my whole heart. May you grow into the woman you dream of becoming without ever losing the softness and sparkle that make you you.

Twenty looks beautiful on you already. This is your new chapter, and I hope it's full of love.

I love you, always. ♡

your forever sister ♡
